How to play

Find the secret movie. You know it is rated somewhere between two movies on IMDb.

  1. Guess any movie. Its IMDb rating is revealed.
  2. If the guess is rated closer to the secret than one of the two bounds, it replaces that bound and the range shrinks.
  3. If it has the exact same rating as the secret movie, the rating gets pinned. Now it is all about which movie it is.
  4. A guess outside the current range teaches you nothing. Try to avoid those.
  5. You have 15 guesses. Hints unlock along the way:
After 2 guessesGenre
After 5 guessesRelease year
After 8 guessesDirector
After 11 guessesFeatured actors
After 14 guessesPlot keywords

🔼 new upper bound 🔽 new lower bound 🟰 same rating ⬜ outside the range 🎯 correct
